## 5. Weather policy (outdoor sessions)

West Palm Beach weather changes fast, especially June to September afternoons and during hurricane season (June 1 to November 30).

- Daniella makes the weather call **by 9:00 a.m. on the day of the session** (or **the evening before** for sunrise sessions) `[PLACEHOLDER]`, using the hourly forecast.
- Weather reschedules are **always free** and don't count as your one free reschedule.
- Light clouds or a passing drizzle are fine and often flattering. Sessions go ahead unless there's **lightning, steady rain, or a tropical-storm or hurricane watch**, or the hourly chance of rain is above about **40%** `[PLACEHOLDER]`.
- If weather interrupts a session already in progress after at least **50%** `[PLACEHOLDER]` of the time, you keep the images captured. Otherwise you get a free make-up session.
- Holiday Minis have **one rain date** announced in advance `[PLACEHOLDER]`.
